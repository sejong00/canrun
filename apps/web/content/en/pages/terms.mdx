## What CanRun is

CanRun is a free website that estimates which open large language models a graphics card, Mac or PC can run locally, which quant to download, how much memory each one needs and roughly how fast it will generate text. There are no accounts and nothing to buy on the site, and the calculator runs in your browser.

## Results are estimates

- **Every verdict, speed and memory figure is an estimate for reference.** They are worked out from published specs, real file sizes and public benchmarks, and they can be off.
- **Your own results depend on things CanRun can't see**: driver and software versions, runtime settings, the context length you actually use, other programs taking up memory, and cooling or power limits.
- **That is why speeds are shown as ranges with a confidence label.** How the numbers are made, how far they have been off and where they fall short are on the <SiteLink to="/methodology">How we calculate</SiteLink> page.
- **The data can be out of date or wrong.** The date of the current data is at the bottom of every page. If a number looks wrong, please tell us.

## Your decisions are your own

CanRun can help you compare hardware and models, but nothing on it is advice to buy, sell or invest in anything. Before buying a graphics card or a computer, check the specs and price with the seller and, if you can, results from people who own that hardware. Decisions you make based on CanRun's estimates, including purchases, are yours.

## Outside links and model licenses

- **CanRun links to sites it doesn't run**, such as Hugging Face, Ollama, llama.cpp and hardware makers. Those sites have their own terms and privacy policies, and we can't vouch for their content or for how it changes.
- **Open models come with their makers' licenses.** Some limit commercial use or set other conditions. CanRun shows a short license label for each model, but the license on the model's own page is the one that applies, so read it before you use the model.
- **Product names and trademarks belong to their owners.** CanRun is not affiliated with any GPU maker or model publisher.

## Quoting and reusing our content

- **You are welcome to quote CanRun's numbers or short passages**, for example in a forum post, an article or a video. Please name CanRun, link to the page you quoted and mention that the numbers are estimates.
- **Please ask first before copying whole pages or republishing the data in bulk**, for example by scraping the site.
- **The source code and the raw results of our own benchmarks are public** in the [sejong00/canrun repository on GitHub](https://github.com/sejong00/canrun), so anyone can check how the numbers are made. The repository has no open-source license at the moment, so if you want to reuse the code or data beyond quoting it, please ask.
- **Data that comes from other sources**, such as model details, hardware specs and public benchmarks, stays subject to those sources' terms. The Sources section of <SiteLink to="/methodology">How we calculate</SiteLink> lists them.
