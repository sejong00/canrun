The DGX Spark is NVIDIA's desktop built on its GB10 chip. NVIDIA sells it with 128GB, and its partner makers sell the same chip under their own names, such as the ASUS Ascent GX10, the Dell Pro Max with GB10 and the Lenovo ThinkStation PGX. NVIDIA lists six partners for a 64GB configuration: Acer, ASUS, Dell, GIGABYTE, HP and MSI.
