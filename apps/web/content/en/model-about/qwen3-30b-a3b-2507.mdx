This is Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507, the July 2025 update from Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team to the non-thinking mode of Qwen3 30B-A3B. It answers directly with no thinking step, and its model card reports better instruction following, coding, tool use and understanding of long inputs up to its native 256k context. It is a mixture-of-experts model with about 30B parameters, of which about 3B are active per token.
