Solar Open 2 is Upstage's mixture-of-experts model from July 2026: 250B parameters, 15B active per token. It is built for agentic work such as office tasks, documents and coding, with tool calling and adjustable reasoning. It reads up to 1M tokens. Only 12 of its 48 layers keep a KV cache, so the cache is about a quarter of what it would be if every layer used full attention.
