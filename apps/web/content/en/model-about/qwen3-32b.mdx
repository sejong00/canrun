Qwen3 32B is the largest dense model in the Qwen3 family, released by Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team in April 2025. It switches between a default thinking mode for reasoning, math and coding and a non-thinking mode for general chat, with tool calling in both. Its native context is 32k tokens, and the model card explains how to extend it to 128k with YaRN.
