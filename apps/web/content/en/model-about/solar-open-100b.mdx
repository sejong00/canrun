Solar Open 100B is Upstage's flagship model, trained from scratch and released in January 2026. It is a mixture-of-experts model with 102B total and 12B active parameters, aimed at reasoning, instruction following and agentic tasks in Korean and English, with up to 128k tokens of context. Its Upstage Solar License builds on Apache 2.0 and allows commercial use, but derivative model names must begin with Solar.
