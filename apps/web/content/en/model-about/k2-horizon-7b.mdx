K2-Horizon 7B is the mid-sized dense model of the K2-Horizon family, which IFM (Institute of Foundation Models) released in September 2026. Its card calls it a 7B-core model, though the full weights come to about 9B parameters, and reports agentic, coding, long-context and reasoning benchmarks. Thinking is always on, with reasoning_effort setting its depth. The native context is 524,288 tokens, and the Apache 2.0 license allows commercial use.
