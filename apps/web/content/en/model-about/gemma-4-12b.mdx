Gemma 4 12B is a dense model that Google DeepMind added to the Gemma 4 family in June 2026. Google calls it unified because it has no separate vision or audio encoder; it takes text, images and short audio clips with up to a 256k context. It targets reasoning, coding and function calling, its thinking mode is off by default, and the license is Apache 2.0.
