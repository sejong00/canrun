Spark-X2.5 4B is a compact general-purpose model that the SparkLLM team released in September 2026 alongside a 1.7B version. Its card highlights coding, tool use and agent workflows, more than 200 languages and a native context of up to 1M tokens. Thinking is on by default, the license is Apache 2.0, and it needs llama.cpp `b10828` or Ollama `0.34.1` or later.
