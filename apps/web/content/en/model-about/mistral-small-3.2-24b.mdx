Mistral Small 3.2 24B is a June 2025 update of Mistral AI's Small 3.1 that follows precise instructions better, repeats itself less and has a more robust function-calling template. It keeps 3.1's image input, 128k context and support for dozens of languages, Korean included. It is released under Apache 2.0, which allows commercial use.
