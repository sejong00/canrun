EXAONE 4.0 32B is the mid-size model of LG AI Research's EXAONE 4.0 series, released in July 2025. It has a regular mode and a reasoning mode you turn on per request, supports tool calling, and works in Korean, English and Spanish with up to 128k tokens of context. Its EXAONE license is non-commercial: research and education only, unless LG grants a commercial license.
