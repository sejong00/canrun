Granite 4.2 30B is a dense reasoning model IBM released in August 2026, aimed at reasoning, code generation, tool calling, agentic workflows and multilingual chat. It thinks before answering by default, and the chat template can switch it to a non-thinking or low-effort mode per request. IBM lists Korean among its 12 tested languages, and the Apache 2.0 license allows commercial use.
