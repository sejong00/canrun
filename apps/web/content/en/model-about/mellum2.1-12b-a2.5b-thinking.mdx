Mellum2.1 12B-A2.5B Thinking is a mixture-of-experts model that JetBrains released in October 2026 for coding agents and for hard coding, math and reasoning problems. About 2.5B of its roughly 12B parameters are active per token, it takes text only, and its context reaches 131,072 tokens. Thinking mode is on by default. The Apache 2.0 license allows commercial use.
