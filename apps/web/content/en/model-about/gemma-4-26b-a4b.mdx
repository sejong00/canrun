Gemma 4 26B-A4B is the mixture-of-experts model in Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 family, released in April 2026. It has about 26B parameters and activates about 4B per token, but all the weights still have to fit in VRAM or system RAM. It reads text and images with up to a 256k context, targets reasoning, coding and function calling, keeps thinking off by default and is licensed under Apache 2.0.
