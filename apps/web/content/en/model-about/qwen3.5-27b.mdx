Qwen3.5 27B is the dense model that Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team released in February 2026 next to the 35B-A3B and 122B-A10B mixture-of-experts models. It reads images and video as well as text, thinks before answering by default, and supports tool calling for agent work. Three of every four layers use linear attention, which keeps its KV cache small at long contexts.
