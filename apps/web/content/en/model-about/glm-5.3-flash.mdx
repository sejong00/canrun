GLM-5.3-Flash is Z.ai's first natively multimodal model in the GLM-5 series, taking images as well as text. It is a mixture-of-experts model with 320B total and 18B active parameters, and its card says a hybrid of sparse and linear attention sharply cuts long-context serving costs. The thinking budget is set with reasoning_effort, which takes low, high or max and defaults to max. The weights are MIT licensed.
