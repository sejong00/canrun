Kanana 1.5 8B is a Korean-English bilingual model from Kakao, released in May 2025. Kakao says this version improves coding, math and function calling over earlier Kanana models; it handles 32k tokens natively and up to 128k with YaRN. It is released under Apache 2.0, which allows commercial use.
