Gemma 4 E2B is the smaller of the two edge models in Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 family, released in April 2026 for laptops and mobile devices. Its model card counts 2.3B effective parameters, and per-layer embedding tables used only for lookups bring the total to about 5.1B. Like E4B, it takes text, images and audio with up to a 128k context, keeps thinking off unless the system prompt starts with `<|think|>`, and is licensed under Apache 2.0.
