Gemma 4 E4B is one of the two edge models in Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 family, released in April 2026 for laptops and mobile devices. The E stands for effective: the model card counts 4.5B effective parameters, and per-layer embedding tables used only for lookups bring the total to about 8B. It takes text, images and audio with up to a 128k context, keeps thinking off unless the system prompt starts with `<|think|>`, and is licensed under Apache 2.0.
