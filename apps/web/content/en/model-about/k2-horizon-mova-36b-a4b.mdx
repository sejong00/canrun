K2-Horizon MoVA 36B-A4B is the mixture-of-experts member of the K2-Horizon family, which IFM released in September 2026. About 4B of its roughly 37B parameters are active per token, and its attention uses a design the card calls Mixture-of-Values (MoVA). Thinking is always on, with reasoning_effort setting its depth, and the native context is 524,288 tokens. The Apache 2.0 license allows commercial use.
