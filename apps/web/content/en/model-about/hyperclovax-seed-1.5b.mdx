HyperCLOVA X SEED 1.5B is a small text model that NAVER released openly in April 2025. NAVER describes it as competitive on Korean language and culture benchmarks, and it supports up to 16k tokens of context. The Hugging Face download is gated: you first accept NAVER's conditions, which include sharing your contact details.
