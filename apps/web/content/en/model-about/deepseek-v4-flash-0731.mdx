DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 is DeepSeek's official release of V4 Flash from July 2026, replacing the preview with substantially stronger agentic skills, according to its card. It is a mixture-of-experts model with about 284B total and about 14B activated parameters, a one-million-token context and an attached speculative decoding module. It answers directly in chat mode or reasons first in thinking mode, with low, high or max reasoning effort. The weights are MIT licensed.
