Gemma 3 12B is an open model from Google DeepMind, released in March 2025 alongside 1B, 4B and 27B sizes. It reads text and images, handles a 128k context and supports over 140 languages; its card lists chat, summarization and image data extraction as uses. Its license is the Gemma Terms of Use with a prohibited-use policy, and the Hugging Face weights are gated until you accept it.
