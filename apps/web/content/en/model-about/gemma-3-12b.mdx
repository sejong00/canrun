Gemma 3 12B is an open model from Google DeepMind, released in March 2025 alongside 1B, 4B and 27B sizes. It reads text and images, handles a 128k context and was pretrained on over 140 languages, with over 35 supported out of the box. Its card lists chat, summarization and image data extraction as uses. Its license is the Gemma Terms of Use with a prohibited-use policy, and the Hugging Face weights are gated until you accept it.
