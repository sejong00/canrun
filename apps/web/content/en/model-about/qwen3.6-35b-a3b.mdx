Qwen3.6 35B-A3B is the first open-weight Qwen3.6 model, released by Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team in April 2026. It keeps the Qwen3.5 35B-A3B design of about 35B parameters with about 3B active per token, and reads images and video as well as text. Its model card focuses on agentic coding, such as frontend work and repository-level reasoning, and adds an option to keep reasoning from earlier messages.
