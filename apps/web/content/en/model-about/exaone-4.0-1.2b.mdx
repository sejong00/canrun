EXAONE 4.0 1.2B is the small, on-device model of LG AI Research's EXAONE 4.0 series, released in July 2025 under a non-commercial license. It keeps the series' optional reasoning mode and tool calling, works in Korean, English and Spanish, and reads up to 64k tokens. For Korean chat, LG suggests `temperature=0.1` so the model does not mix in other languages.
