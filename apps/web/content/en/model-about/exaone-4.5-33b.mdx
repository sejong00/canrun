EXAONE 4.5 33B, released in April 2026, is LG AI Research's first open-weight vision language model: it adds a vision encoder to the EXAONE 4.0 design, so it reads images as well as text. LG highlights document understanding and Korean contextual reasoning; thinking is on by default, and it supports tool use and up to 256k tokens of context. The license is non-commercial.
