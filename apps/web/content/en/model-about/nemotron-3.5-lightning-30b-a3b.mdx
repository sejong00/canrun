Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B-A3B is a general-purpose reasoning and chat model NVIDIA released in August 2026, also trained on coding and tool-calling data. It is a mixture-of-experts model that mixes Mamba-2 and attention layers, with about 30B parameters in total but about 3B active per token. Its thinking mode can be switched on or off in the chat template, and the OpenMDW-1.1 license allows commercial use.
