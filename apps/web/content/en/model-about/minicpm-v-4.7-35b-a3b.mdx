MiniCPM-V 4.7 35B-A3B is a vision-language model that OpenBMB released in October 2026, the first mixture-of-experts model in the MiniCPM-V series. It accepts one or more images and video, and its model card includes guides for OCR and PDF parsing. Thinking mode is on by default, and about 3B of its 35B parameters are active per token.
