LFM2.5 2.6B is a small hybrid model that Liquid AI released in August 2026 for on-device agents. It is text-only with a 128k context and 16 languages including Korean, and Liquid AI recommends it for tool use, data extraction and RAG but not agentic coding. It always thinks before answering, and its LFM Open License 1.0 excludes commercial use by companies earning 10 million US dollars or more a year.
