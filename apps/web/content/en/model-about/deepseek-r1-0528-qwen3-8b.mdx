DeepSeek R1 0528 Qwen3 8B is a reasoning model that DeepSeek released in May 2025. DeepSeek post-trained Qwen3 8B Base on chain-of-thought data from its upgraded DeepSeek-R1-0528, and the architecture is the same as Qwen3 8B. Unlike the January distills, it accepts a system prompt, and the model card recommends a temperature of 0.6. The MIT license allows commercial use.
