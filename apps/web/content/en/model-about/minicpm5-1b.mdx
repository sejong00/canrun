MiniCPM5 1B is the first model in OpenBMB's MiniCPM5 series, released in May 2026. Its model card aims it at on-device and local use and names tool use, code generation and harder reasoning as strengths. The same checkpoint can answer with or without a thinking step, and official GGUF files are available for llama.cpp, Ollama and LM Studio.
