Gemma 3 27B is the largest of the four Gemma 3 sizes that Google DeepMind released in March 2025. It reads text and images with a 128k context and supports over 140 languages, and the release post adds function calling and structured output for agent tasks. Its license is the Gemma Terms of Use with a prohibited-use policy, and the Hugging Face weights are gated until you accept it.
