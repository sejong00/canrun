Qwen3.5 35B-A3B is a mixture-of-experts model that Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team released in February 2026, with about 35B parameters in total and about 3B active per token. It reads images and video as well as text, thinks before answering by default, and its model card highlights coding, agents and support for 201 languages and dialects. The card names Qwen3.5-Flash on Alibaba Cloud as its hosted version.
