Qwen3.5 9B is the largest of the small Qwen3.5 models that Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team released in March 2026. It reads images and video as well as text, thinks before answering by default, and its model card highlights coding, agents, tool calling and 201 languages and dialects. Only one layer in four uses full attention, keeping the KV cache small even at its native 256k context.
