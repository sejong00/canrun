Phi-4 is a 14B dense model from Microsoft Research, released in December 2024. It was trained on a blend of synthetic "textbook-like" data, filtered web data and academic books, with a focus on reasoning and logic such as math and coding. Microsoft aims it mainly at English, its context window is 16k tokens, and the MIT license allows commercial use.
