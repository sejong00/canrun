Qwen3.5 122B-A10B is a mixture-of-experts model that Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team released in February 2026 alongside the 27B and 35B-A3B. It reads images and video as well as text, thinks before answering by default, and supports tool calling. Only about 10B of its parameters are active per token, but all of its weights still need to fit in memory.
