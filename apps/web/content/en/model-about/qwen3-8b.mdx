Qwen3 8B is a dense model that Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team released in April 2025. It thinks step by step before every answer by default, which helps with math, coding and logic, and thinking can be switched off for quick everyday chat. Its model card lists more than 100 languages and tool calling for agent tasks, and the Apache 2.0 license allows commercial use.
