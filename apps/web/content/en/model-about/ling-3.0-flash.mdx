Ling-3.0 Flash is the larger sibling of Ling-3.0 Tiny, released in August 2026 by inclusionAI, Ant Group's AGI initiative. This mixture-of-experts model activates 5.1B parameters per token and targets coding, agent workflows, reasoning and contexts up to 256k. Only a small share of its weights is used for each token, but all of them must fit in VRAM or system RAM. Thinking is on by default, and the license is MIT.
