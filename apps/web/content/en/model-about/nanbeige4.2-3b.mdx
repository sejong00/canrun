Nanbeige4.2 3B is a compact agentic model that Nanbeige LLM Lab released in July 2026. It is a looped transformer that reuses its layers to add capacity without adding parameters, with 3B non-embedding and 4B total parameters. Its card highlights general-agent, code-agent and reasoning tasks. Thinking is on by default and enable_thinking turns it off; the context reaches 262,144 tokens, and the Apache 2.0 license allows commercial use.
