## How sure are these numbers?

Memory figures come by formula from real GGUF file sizes and each model's published configuration, so they carry no error band. Prices are the least stable numbers here: street prices change, and ours are a dated snapshot.

Speeds are estimates with a band and a label. Measured means a public benchmark for that card, model and quant. Calibrated means ±12% for dense models fully on an NVIDIA or AMD card, and ±20% for MoE models, partial offload and other hardware such as Intel Arc. Theoretical means ±30%, for setups whose real speed varies widely, such as MoE experts in system RAM.

All verdicts assume Windows with the card driving the display, 32GB of DDR5-5600 dual-channel system RAM, an 8k context, an f16 KV cache, one model and one request at a time. Ollama and llama.cpp make their own memory estimates, so a model that fits with little margin here can end up partly in system RAM; the PROCESSOR column of `ollama ps` shows what happened.
